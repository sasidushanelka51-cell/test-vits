President's message

Photography is more than simply pressing a shutter. It is about learning to see — to notice the moments, emotions, people, and details that others may pass by. Every photograph has a story behind it, and every photographer has a unique way of telling that story.

As the President of the St. Sylvester’s College Photography Club, I am proud to be part of a community of students who share a passion for capturing and creating visual stories. Our club is a place where creativity meets learning, where beginners can discover their abilities, and where experienced members can continue to challenge themselves and grow.

Throughout our journey, we hope to create opportunities for every member to explore photography and videography in their own way. Through practical sessions, workshops, competitions, exhibitions, school events, creative projects, and collaboration, we want to turn our passion into skills that can be carried far beyond the classroom.

But a photography club is not built only around cameras and photographs. It is built around people. The ideas we share, the experiences we create together, the lessons we learn from one another, and the friendships we build along the way are what make this community meaningful.

I encourage every member to experiment without being afraid of mistakes. Try different perspectives. Explore new techniques. Tell stories that matter to you. Not every photograph needs to be perfect — sometimes the most valuable part is what you learn while creating it.

My vision for the club is to build a space where every student feels encouraged to create, contribute, and express themselves. I hope we can continue developing not only as photographers and videographers, but also as responsible, creative, and confident individuals.

Let us capture the moments of today, preserve the memories of our school, and create a visual legacy that future generations of St. Sylvester’s College can look back on with pride.
Keep creating. Keep exploring. Keep seeing the world differently.

Sasidu Shanelka

What is Spectrum

The event

Spectrum

Spectrum is the flagship photography event organized by the Photography Club of St. Sylvester’s College, Kandy. The event begins with an island-wide online photography competition, where participants submit their photographs through a Google Form under various categories. The selected and winning photographs are then showcased at a photography exhibition held at the college premises during the main event.

The Photography Day features an exhibition, an interactive session conducted by two renowned professional photographers serving as the Chief Guest and Guest of Honour, and a certificate award ceremony recognizing the competition winners. Souvenirs are also presented to the distinguished guests, the Principal, the MIC, and past presidents of the Photography Club. Through this event, Spectrum aims to celebrate creativity, encourage young photographers across Sri Lanka, and promote the art of photography within the school community.

Competition

Online Photography Competition

The Spectrum Online Photography Competition is an island-wide inter-school photography competition open to students from schools across Sri Lanka. The competition features five categories: Colour, Monochrome, Nature, Wildlife, and Mobile photography. Each participant may submit up to three photographs in each category.

The competition commenced on 14 August and will remain open until 30 September (extended from 20 September). A total of eight winners will be selected, including 1st place, 2nd place, 3rd place, and five merit award recipients. The winning and selected photographs will be displayed at the Spectrum Photography Exhibition, and the winners will be recognized during the certificate award ceremony held on the main Photography Day.

Exhibition

Photography Exhibition

The Spectrum Photography Exhibition is the main highlight of Spectrum Photography Day, held on 14 October 2026 at the Open Hall of St. Sylvester’s College, Kandy. The exhibition will feature the winning and selected photographs from the Spectrum island-wide inter-school photography competition, as chosen by the official judging panel. Students, participants, guests, and visitors will have the opportunity to view the exhibition throughout the event, celebrating the creativity and talent of young photographers from across Sri Lanka.

Main event

The Photography Day

The Spectrum Photography Day will be held on 14 October at the Cingolani Memorial Hall, St. Sylvester’s College, Kandy. The event will serve as the main celebration of Spectrum, bringing together students, participants, and distinguished guests for a day dedicated to the art of photography.

The programme will feature a photography exhibition showcasing selected and winning photographs from the island-wide online competition, educational lectures and interactive sessions conducted by two renowned professional photographers serving as the Chief Guest and Guest of Honour, and a certificate award ceremony recognizing the competition winners. The guest lectures will provide valuable insights into photography techniques, creativity, visual storytelling, and career opportunities in the field of photography. Souvenirs will also be presented to the distinguished guests, the Principal, the MIC, and past presidents of the Photography Club.
